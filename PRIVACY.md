# Privacy

Universal Video Downloader has no user account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or application backend in the core release.

The app sends the URL you submit to the video host you selected so Android can download the requested file. A lightweight metadata request may be made to that URL to determine whether it appears to be a direct video file. Downloaded files remain on the device unless you share or move them.

The app does not request access to contacts, messages, microphone, camera, or location.

Do not submit private URLs, authentication tokens, or content you are not authorized to download.
